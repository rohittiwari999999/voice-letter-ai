# VoiceLetter AI - Google Play Console & Release Assets

All files required for publishing and testing on Google Play Console have been compiled, packaged, and saved in this directory:

---

### 📦 1. Application Packages (`play_console_assets/builds/`)
- **`VoiceLetter_AI_v1.0.aab`**: Android App Bundle (`.aab`) ready to be uploaded to the **Internal Testing / Closed Testing / Production Track** on Google Play Console.
- **`VoiceLetter_AI_v1.0.apk`**: Direct installable Android package (`.apk`) for manual testing, side-loading, or sharing with test devices.

---

### 🎨 2. Play Store Graphic Assets (`play_console_assets/graphics/`)
- **`play_store_icon_512x512.png`**: High-resolution 512x512 PNG icon matching the official Play Store listing icon specification (32-bit RGBA, no alpha border).
- **`feature_graphic_1024x500.png`**: 1024x500 PNG Feature Graphic banner required under the "Main store listing" section.

---

### 📝 3. Store Listing Copy (`play_console_assets/store_listing/`)
- **`app_title.txt`**: App name (14 chars, under the 30-char Play Store limit).
- **`short_description.txt`**: Short promotional summary (79 chars, under the 80-char limit).
- **`full_description.txt`**: Comprehensive description detailing multilingual speech-to-text, live A4 formatting, templates, and PDF/DOCX export.
- **`release_notes.txt`**: What's new in v1.0 in English and Hindi.
- **`data_safety_guide.txt`**: Field-by-field answers for the Play Console Data Safety questionnaire.

---

### 🔒 4. Legal & Policy (`play_console_assets/`)
- **`privacy_policy.md`**: Complete Privacy Policy compliant with Google Play Developer Policies. Host this document or paste into a public page (e.g. GitHub Pages or your website) and provide the URL in the Play Console **App Content > Privacy policy** section.
