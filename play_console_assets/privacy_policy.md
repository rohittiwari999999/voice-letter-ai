# Privacy Policy for VoiceLetter AI

**Last Updated:** October 06, 2026

Welcome to **VoiceLetter AI** ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information processed through the Application.

This Privacy Policy explains how our Application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our service.

---

### 1. Information Collection & Permissions

#### A. Audio & Microphone Access (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`)
- **Purpose:** The Application uses the microphone strictly when you explicitly tap the voice recording button to transcribe your speech into letter text and process voice formatting commands (e.g., *"Make the subject bold"*).
- **Processing:** Audio is processed in real time via the on-device / operating system speech recognition engine or secure AI processing. We **do not store**, sell, or retain raw voice audio recordings on external servers.

#### B. Document Storage & Local Database
- **Local Storage:** All letters, drafts, templates, and document revisions created by you are stored locally on your device using a local SQLite database (Room).
- **User Control:** You have full control to view, edit, export (PDF, DOCX, TXT), or delete your letters at any time.

#### C. Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`)
- Used exclusively for communication with the AI generation and translation service (e.g., Google Gemini API) to generate letter structures and translations.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services
The Application may utilize Google Cloud / Google AI APIs for natural language letter generation and translation. These services process text prompts according to Google's standard enterprise privacy guidelines and do not use customer inputs to train public foundation models without consent.

---

### 3. Data Retention and Security
We do not create user accounts, require logins, or maintain persistent profiles on third-party servers. Your generated documents remain private on your device unless you choose to export or share them via third-party apps (e.g., Gmail, WhatsApp, Drive).

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
The Application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted within the application and in the store listing.
